# Referral growth research: what makes people share and friends convert

Updated: 2026-11-20
What this is for: an evidence base for choosing features of an open-source Flutter + Supabase referral kit (subscription apps, India and WhatsApp first). Each claim has a link and an evidence-strength label.
Related: [compliance re-check](referral-compliance.md) · [ADR 0001](../adr/0001-referral-kit.md) · [TODO](../TODO.md)

## How to read the strength labels

- **Primary**: a peer-reviewed paper, a randomised field experiment, the company's own engineering or product writing, the company's official terms or help page, or a court or regulator record.
- **Secondary**: a vendor or growth blog, press coverage, or a talk summary that repeats a number. Treat these numbers as unverified.
- **None**: no source found. The idea is a hypothesis, not a finding.

### Limits of this research

- Wharton PDFs (Schmitt 2011, Van den Bulte 2018) and the NBER paper downloaded as binary, and no PDF text tool was available. Their numbers come from the authors' own institution summaries (Wharton Knowledge, the Wharton-hosted later paper) and from abstracts.
- Airbnb's Medium posts returned 403. Airbnb facts come from search excerpts of the post and from Lenny Rachitsky (ex-Airbnb). They are marked accordingly.
- No primary source was found for these: a WhatsApp-vs-SMS referral conversion rate in India, a lift from showing the inviter's photo on a landing page, a lift from reminding invited friends, or a referral-specific test of a tiered ladder. Wherever these come up below, the report says so.

## 1. Table of mechanics

| # | Mechanic | Who did it | Evidence + link | Strength | How a kit could support it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Friend-benefiting ("give") rewards beat sender-only rewards** | GiftAMeal field test (6,364 customers: control / sender $5 / friend $5 / split / charity) | Gershon, Cryder & John, *JMR* 57 (2020): in 2 field experiments and 1 incentivised lab study, recipient-benefiting incentives recruited more new customers than sender-benefiting ones. Proposed reasons: reputation for the sender, plus a lower cost for the friend to act. In the field test the gap was only *marginal* and conversion was low overall. Of 351 programs surveyed, 40.5% were sender-only, 2.6% recipient-only and 55% shared. [WashU summary](https://source.washu.edu/2020/07/research-shows-how-businesses-can-win-more-customers-influence-your-friends/) · [paper record](https://profiles.wustl.edu/en/publications/why-prosocial-referral-incentives-work-the-interplay-of-reputatio/) | Primary | Always give the friend a reward. Make "friend gets X" the headline of the share screen. |
| 2 | **Pro-social wording of the call to action** | Unnamed large e-commerce platform; randomised test on 100,000 customers | Jung, Bapna, Golden & Sun, *Information Systems Research*. The pro-social CTA (e.g. "gift her $20 off her first bill") raised the share of people who made any referral by 60%, total referrals by 86% and new-customer conversion by 86%, compared with control. The mechanism was lower guilt about sending. [Wharton Knowledge](https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/article/how-to-increase-word-of-mouth-referrals/) | Primary | Default copy templates use "Give your friend…" rather than "Earn…". Allow per-locale strings (hi/mr). |
| 3 | **Altruistic email framing at Airbnb** | Airbnb (Bosinoff, 2014) | A/B test of "earn $25 for inviting a friend" against "share $25 with a friend". The altruistic version won globally. The post says the program raised signups and bookings per day by over 300%. The baseline was not visible. [Airbnb Tech Blog post](https://medium.com/airbnb-engineering/hacking-word-of-mouth-making-referrals-work-for-airbnb-46468e7790a6) (403 to fetch; seen via [search summary](https://growsurf.com/blog/airbnb-referral-program/)) | Primary claim, read through secondary excerpts | Same as #2. |
| 4 | **Uncertain reward for the sender, guaranteed reward for the friend** | Chinese telecom operator; randomised, >160,000 users, 2 months | Li, Belo & Li, *Management Science* (online 26 Feb 2026). Making the sender's reward uncertain raised total referrals by 20.9%. Making the recipient's reward uncertain *cut* referrals by 12.3%, and those recipients later engaged less and referred less. Mechanism: uncertainty reduces the sender's guilt, but feels unfair and pressuring to the recipient. [paper](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/full/10.1287/mnsc.2024.05685) · [RSM summary](https://www.rsm.nl/discovery/2026/acceptable-social-referral-campaigns/) | Primary | Optional "surprise bonus" (scratch card) for the **sender only**. The friend's welcome gift is always fixed and shown up front. |
| 5 | **Referred customers are worth more** | German bank, ~10,000 customers over ~3 years | Schmitt, Skiera & Van den Bulte, *J. Marketing* 75 (2011). Referred customers had 16% higher lifetime value over a 6-year horizon and churned about 18% more slowly. Their margin advantage faded to zero after about 1,000 days, but the retention advantage did not. Estimated ROI was about 60% on a €25 reward. [Wharton Knowledge](https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/article/turning-social-capital-into-economic-capital-straight-talk-about-word-of-mouth-marketing/) · [paper PDF](https://faculty.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2012/04/Schmitt-Skiera-vandenBulte-2011-Referral-Programs-Customer-Value.pdf) · [GfK MIR summary](https://faculty.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/Schmitt_Skiera_VandenBulte_2013_Referrral_Programs_2.pdf) | Primary | Cohort report: referred vs non-referred retention and revenue, so each app can check whether this holds for it. |
| 6 | **Who makes the best referrers** | Same bank, 1,799 referred customers | Van den Bulte, Bayer, Skiera & Schmitt, *JMR* 55(1) (2018). The paper tests two explanations: better matching, and social enrichment through the referrer's tie. For this bank, the best referrers were acquired at least 6 months earlier, had high margins and were unlikely to churn. [paper PDF](https://faculty.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/VdB-et-al.-JMR-2018.pdf) · [AMA summary](https://www.ama.org/2019/11/20/maximizing-the-benefits-of-customer-referrals/) | Primary (via abstract and summaries) | Eligibility rules for who is prompted: tenure, active, paying. Prompt engaged and paying users more often. |
| 7 | **Referred customers refer more ("referral contagion")** | SaaSquatch data from 20 companies; a field test with >10M referred customers | Gershon & Jiang, *JMR* 62(1) (2024). Referred customers bring in more customers than similar non-referred ones (30–50% more by the authors' podcast account; another transcript says 57%). This held at 19 of 20 companies, mostly because they *send more invites*. Reminding them "You were referred – now refer your friends!" gave a ~20% increase in referred customers. [paper PDF](https://faculty.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/gershon-jiang-2024-express-referral-contagion-downstream-benefits-of-customer-referrals.pdf) · [AMA](https://www.ama.org/2026/03/02/referral-contagion-capturing-the-full-roi-of-referral-programs/) · [Wharton podcast](https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/podcast/knowledge-at-wharton-podcast/the-trick-to-boosting-customer-referrals/) | Primary | Flag `was_referred`. Show referred users their own "you joined through X, now pass it on" prompt after they activate. |
| 8 | **Taking part in referral makes the referrer stay** | Global mobile telecom | Garnefeld, Eggert, Helm & Tax, *J. Marketing* 77(4) (2013). Among recommenders, defection over a year fell from 19% to 7%, and monthly revenue rose 11.4% against a matched control. The effect was strongest for newer customers. In the lab, larger rewards raised attitudinal *and* behavioural loyalty, while small rewards raised only behavioural loyalty. [ASU summary](https://csl.wpcarey.asu.edu/growing-existing-customers-revenue-streams-through-customer-referral-programs) · [record](https://ris.uni-paderborn.de/record/4851) | Primary | Count referral as a retention feature too. Report the referrers' own churn. |
| 9 | **Reward size: more customers, less profitable ones** | Bank (>160,000 customers, field experiment) and telecom (~270,000, archival) | Wolters, Schulze & Gedenk, *Marketing Science* 39(6) (2020). Larger rewards brought more referred customers, but those customers were considerably less profitable. [record](https://econbiz.de/Record/referral-reward-size-and-new-customer-profitability-wolters-heike-maria/10012405758) · [INFORMS](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/fpi/10.1287/mksc.2020.1242) | Primary | Use a moderate default reward. Run reward size as an experiment arm, and measure activation and paid conversion, not just signups. |
| 10 | **Rewards help weaker brands more** | Lab experiments | Ryu & Feick, *J. Marketing* 71(1) (2007). A reward raised referral likelihood by more than 20 points for the weaker brand and less than 10 points for the stronger brand. For strong ties and strong brands, the abstract says giving at least part of the reward to the receiver works better. [Baylor Keller Center](https://kellercenter.hankamer.baylor.edu/news/story/2008/insider-referral-reward-programs-and-referral-likelihood) · [record](https://searchworks.jioinstitute.edu.in/articles/edb__23368367) | Primary (through abstract and summary; sources disagree on the weak-tie detail) | Small or new apps need a reward more than well-known ones. Explain this in the docs. |
| 11 | **Rewarded referrals make friends suspicious** | Experiments | Verlegh, Ryu, Tuk & Feick, *JAMS* 41(6) (2013). Rewarded referrals get less favourable responses because receivers infer ulterior motives. Rewarding *both* sides, or using *symbolic* rewards, removes the negative effect. [record](https://dare.uva.nl/id/553a0430-0ee6-4f93-bd73-f97db1105ca2) | Primary | Keep the program double-sided. The share message says "you get X too", not "I get paid". Status badges can stand in for cash. |
| 12 | **Double-sided storage reward** | Dropbox (2008–2010) | Houston's slides: a two-sided referral program "permanently increased signups by 60%". 2.8M direct invites were sent in the trailing 30 days (Apr 2010). One copy of the deck says 35% of daily signups came from referrals. [slides](https://www.slideshare.net/gueste94e4c/dropbox-startup-lessons-learned-3836587). Current terms: 500 MB to each side on Basic, capped at 16 GB; the friend must install the app. [Dropbox help](https://help.dropbox.com/storage-space/how-much-free-space) | Primary (founder's slides as reposted on SlideShare; official help page) | In-product reward (free days), the same unit on both sides, with a lifetime cap. |
| 13 | **Reward on the friend's first real action, inside a time window** | Google Pay India | The referrer gets ₹101 when the friend makes their first payment within 28 days, plus ₹25 a week for 4 weeks of eligible payments. The friend gets ₹21, plus ₹5–₹50 per eligible payment. Caps: 100 rewards and ₹9,000 per financial year. Ineligible: previously registered number, email or bank; refurbished or second-hand phones used before; recycled numbers; non-+91 numbers; installing directly instead of via the link. [Google Pay help](https://support.google.com/pay/india/answer/13267498?hl=en) | Primary (official terms) | The kit already rewards on activation with a 7-day claim window. Add an optional weekly follow-up quest for both sides, device and number dedupe, and a yearly cap. |
| 14 | **Activation-gated rewards bring better users** | CRED (India) | AppsFlyer case study: both sides are rewarded only after the friend completes the required in-app actions. Referred users had a 50% higher eligibility rate and a 44% higher first-payment rate than average. Invites are deep links, not codes. [AppsFlyer](https://www.appsflyer.com/customers/cred/) | Secondary (vendor case study) | Same as #13. |
| 15 | **Milestone ladder** | Robinhood US; Harry's pre-launch | Robinhood's current tiers pay $50 at 1 referral, $150 at 3, $800 at 10 and $4,000 at 50, capped at $15,000 a year. [Robinhood](https://robinhood.com/us/en/referrals). Harry's collected ~100k emails in a week, 77% of them from referrals, with milestone prizes. [Tim Ferriss post by the founders](https://tim.blog/2014/07/21/harrys-prelaunch/). No referral-specific experiment on ladders was found. The closest science is the goal gradient: Kivetz, Urminsky & Zheng, *JMR* (2006). Coffee-card customers bought faster near the reward, and a 12-stamp card with 2 free stamps was finished faster than a 10-stamp card (median 10 vs 15 days). [Columbia](https://business.columbia.edu/faculty/research/goal-gradient-hypothesis-resurrected-purchase-acceleration-illusionary-goal) | Program terms: primary. Effect of ladders on referrals: **none** (goal gradient is primary, but not about referrals) | Send referral events to the rewards engine's milestones. Show "2 of 3 friends joined". Consider a head start (count the first activated friend toward tier 1). |
| 16 | **"Invite N friends to unlock premium"** | Online dating platform; randomised referral thresholds | Belo & Li, *Management Science* 68(12) (2022). Stricter referral thresholds were more effective for platform growth. The RSM summary says the program raised referrals but **not revenue** (more referrals, fewer paying users). [RSM](https://rsm.nl/discovery/2022/engagement-dating-apps) · [RePEc](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:inm:ormnsc:v:68:y:2022:i:12:p:8933-8962) | Primary | For a subscription app, keep free-days rewards modest and *time-limited*, so they don't replace paying. Watch Apple 3.2.2 / 5.1.1(v) (row 23). |
| 17 | **Personalised invite (friend's name shown)** | Facebook ads; gym | Bakshy et al., ACM EC 2012, field experiments on ~5.7M users: even a minimal cue showing one friend's affiliation raised ad performance (clicks and connections), most for strong ties. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.4327). Stanford gym study: a personalised message raised referrals across the board. [Stanford GSB](https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/insights/personal-touch-quantified). Airbnb tested personalised referral codes and URLs (no numbers seen). No test of inviter photo on a referral landing page was found. | Primary for "a friend cue helps"; none for the exact landing-page design | `/i/CODE` and the first app screen show "Priya invited you" with name and avatar, opt-in by the inviter. |
| 18 | **Broadcast vs personal invites** | Facebook app; 9,687 users and their 1.4M friends | Aral & Walker, *Management Science* 57(9) (2011). Passive broadcast features raised peer influence by 246%. Adding active, personalised invites added only a further 98%: they convert better per message, but are sent far less often. [paper](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/fpi/10.1287/mnsc.1110.1421) · [MIT SMR](https://sloanreview.mit.edu/?p=12601) | Primary | Besides 1:1 WhatsApp shares, offer a one-tap "post to WhatsApp Status / story" share card (broadcast). |
| 19 | **WhatsApp prefilled share** | WhatsApp official | `https://wa.me/?text=<urlencoded>` opens WhatsApp with the text prefilled and lets the user pick contacts. [WhatsApp FAQ](https://whatsapp.com/faq/en/general/26000030). India WhatsApp-vs-SMS conversion: only an unsourced vendor claim of "4–5x". [productgrowth.in](https://productgrowth.in/insights/consumer/referral-program-design-india/) | Mechanism: primary. Conversion lift: **none** (unverified vendor claim) | A dedicated WhatsApp button above the generic share sheet. Message under ~300 characters, with the link early so the preview renders. Measure the channel per share. |
| 20 | **Attribution through install** | Google Play | The Install Referrer API returns the referrer URL for 90 days after install, unchanged unless the app is reinstalled. Call it once on first run. [Android docs](https://developer.android.com/google/play/installreferrer/igetinstallreferrerservice). Google Pay excludes friends who install without the link (row 13), which is why seamless attribution matters. | Primary | Already in the kit. Add a fallback "Got a code?" entry and a server-side match window. |
| 21 | **Deep-link carry-through** | Branch / AppsFlyer customers | Vendor case studies: The League saw +31% referral conversions and Wheely 8x installs with auto-applied promo codes. [Branch](https://www.branch.io/resources/blog/deep-linking-the-unsung-hero-of-mobile-growth/) | Secondary (vendor, unverified) | Auto-apply the code. Never make the friend type it. |
| 22 | **Referral program as a top growth channel** | Airbnb; PayPal | Lenny Rachitsky (ex-Airbnb): the host referral program was "the single biggest driver of attributable supply growth". Where referrals work, 15–50% of new users come from them; expect <10% of users to make a successful referral. A tiny reward (+$10) likely won't move people, and doubling the reward sometimes changed little. [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/this-week-16-building-a-referrals). PayPal's referral bonus cost roughly $60–70M by Musk's estimate. [summary](https://www.referralcandy.com/blog/paypal) | Lenny: practitioner first-hand (semi-primary). PayPal figure: secondary | Docs: "referrals amplify existing word of mouth; check you have it first". Ship the metric set in #26. |
| 23 | **Platform rules on invites and contacts** | Apple | 5.1.1(v): "inviting friends to use the app are not considered core app functionality" (so you cannot force sign-in for it). 5.1.2(i): you may not require enabling push, location or tracking to "receive monetary or other compensation". 5.1.2(iv)/(v): don't build a contact database; contact people from Contacts only at the user's explicit, individual initiative; no Select All; show the message and sender first. 5.1.1(iii): prefer the share sheet or picker over full Contacts access. 3.2.2(x): no forcing store actions (ratings, downloads) for access; in-app actions may be incentivised. [Apple guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/). Developers report 3.2.2 rejections over rewards to the *referee*. [forum](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/674832) | Guidelines: primary. Rejection anecdotes: secondary | No contacts permission at all: use the share sheet and wa.me. The friend's reward is in-app value, not tied to rating or installing other apps. Gate nothing behind push opt-in. |
| 24 | **Status / symbolic rewards** | Research | Symbolic rewards remove the suspicion penalty of paid referrals (Verlegh et al. 2013, row 11). No primary data was found on badges or leaderboards increasing referrals. Tesla's car-prize leaderboard was gamed by influencers and then cut for cost (row A4). | Primary (suspicion effect); none (badges driving volume) | Optional "Founding member" or "Brought 5 friends" badge in the rewards engine. No public leaderboard of cash amounts. |
| 25 | **Timing of the ask** | Registered trial; charity giving | No published referral-timing result was found. An AEA-registered credit-card trial (1 day vs 1 week after a "happy moment") has no public data yet. [AEA registry](https://www.socialscienceregistry.org/trials/14626). In a hospital-donation field study, positive reciprocity decayed roughly linearly with time ("faster was simply better"). [PNAS 2018](https://faculty.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/2018_PNAS.pdf) | Weak: primary but indirect | Keep "ask after a moment of value" (already in the kit) and fire it soon after the moment. Make the moment configurable per app, and A/B test it. |
| 26 | **Funnel / K-factor metrics** | Andrew Chen; Airbnb | K = invites per user × invite conversion rate. Below 1 the loop decays; cycle time matters. [andrewchen.com](https://andrewchen.com/more-retention-more-viral-growth/amp/). Airbnb host-referral inputs: landing visits, invites sent (total and per user), invite→signup conversion, cost efficiency. Outputs: new listings and bookings. Account for cannibalisation (some would have joined anyway). [Lenny](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/this-week-16-building-a-referrals) | Practitioner (semi-primary) | Supabase views: prompt shown → share tapped (by channel) → link opened → install → signup → activation → paid; per-cohort retention; an optional holdout group for incrementality. |

## 2. Ranked: the 12 highest-value features for the kit

Ranked by strength of evidence × size of effect × fit for an Indian, WhatsApp-first subscription app. ✔ means the kit already has it; the item notes what to add.

1. **Friend-first reward and "give" copy as the default.** Pro-social CTA: +60% senders and +86% referrals in a 100k-customer RCT ([ISR / Wharton](https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/article/how-to-increase-word-of-mouth-referrals/)). Recipient-benefiting rewards recruit more ([JMR 2020](https://source.washu.edu/2020/07/research-shows-how-businesses-can-win-more-customers-influence-your-friends/)). Airbnb's "share $25" beat "earn $25". *(✔ double-sided; add "give" copy templates in en/hi/mr, with the friend's gift as the headline.)*
2. **Guaranteed, visible welcome gift for the friend; optional surprise bonus for the sender only.** Uncertain sender reward +20.9% referrals; uncertain recipient reward −12.3% ([Mgmt Sci 2026](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/full/10.1287/mnsc.2024.05685)).
3. **Reward on activation, with a window, not on install.** Google Pay pays on the first payment within 28 days ([terms](https://support.google.com/pay/india/answer/13267498?hl=en)). Larger or easier rewards bring less profitable customers ([Marketing Science 2020](https://econbiz.de/Record/referral-reward-size-and-new-customer-profitability-wolters-heike-maria/10012405758)). *(✔ activation + 72 h hold + 7-day claim; add a configurable activation event and the paid-conversion bonus.)*
4. **Abuse controls: one reward per device, number and store account; yearly cap; clawback on refund or quick cancel.** Google Pay excludes recycled numbers and second-hand phones and caps at 100 rewards / ₹9,000 a year. Revolut reverses the reward if the friend closes within 14 days ([terms](https://www.revolut.com/legal/referrals-terms/)) and paused a scheme when it was gamed ([Finextra](https://finextra.com/newsarticle/40123/revolut-suspends-refugee-referral-payouts-after-users-game-the-system)). *(✔ 10 joins/30 days; add device and number dedupe, a lifetime or yearly cap, and a RevenueCat refund → revoke path.)*
5. **"You were referred, now pass it on" prompt for referred users.** Referred customers refer 30–50% more, and this reminder lifted referred customers ~20% in a >10M-customer test ([JMR 2024](https://faculty.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/gershon-jiang-2024-express-referral-contagion-downstream-benefits-of-customer-referrals.pdf)).
6. **Referred-vs-organic cohort and funnel dashboard, with an optional holdout.** Referred customers: +16% LTV and ~18% slower churn, while the margin gap fades by ~1,000 days ([J. Marketing 2011](https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/article/turning-social-capital-into-economic-capital-straight-talk-about-word-of-mouth-marketing/)). Each app must check this for itself, and correct for cannibalisation ([Lenny](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/this-week-16-building-a-referrals)).
7. **Prompt the right referrers: tenured, active or paying users, soon after a value moment.** Best referrers were acquired at least 6 months earlier, had high margins and were unlikely to churn ([JMR 2018](https://www.ama.org/2019/11/20/maximizing-the-benefits-of-customer-referrals/)). Timing evidence is only indirect: reciprocity decays with time ([PNAS 2018](https://faculty.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/2018_PNAS.pdf)). *(✔ moment-of-value prompt, 14-day frequency cap; add eligibility rules.)*
8. **Referral as retention: show referrers their impact and count it as engagement.** Recommenders' defection fell from 19% to 7% and their revenue rose 11.4% ([J. Marketing 2013](https://csl.wpcarey.asu.edu/growing-existing-customers-revenue-streams-through-customer-referral-programs)). *(✔ stats shown from 3 friends; also send "your friend activated" pushes to the referrer.)*
9. **Personalised invite: inviter's name and avatar on `/i/CODE` and on the friend's first screen, with the gift stated.** A single-friend cue raised ad performance in a ~5.7M-user field experiment, most for strong ties ([Bakshy 2012](https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.4327)). Double-sided wording removes suspicion of paid referrals ([JAMS 2013](https://dare.uva.nl/id/553a0430-0ee6-4f93-bd73-f97db1105ca2)). No direct test of a landing page with the inviter's photo was found.
10. **WhatsApp-first share: a 1:1 `wa.me/?text=` button plus a broadcast share card (Status / story).** Passive broadcast drove more total adoption than personal invites (+246% vs a further +98%) ([Mgmt Sci 2011](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/fpi/10.1287/mnsc.1110.1421)). The wa.me mechanism is official ([WhatsApp FAQ](https://whatsapp.com/faq/en/general/26000030)). The India WhatsApp conversion uplift is **not** sourced: measure it per channel.
11. **Milestone ladder with progress ("2 of 3 joined") and a head start, run by the rewards engine.** The goal gradient is primary: the head-start stamp card was finished in median 10 vs 15 days ([JMR 2006](https://business.columbia.edu/faculty/research/goal-gradient-hypothesis-resurrected-purchase-acceleration-illusionary-goal)). Robinhood and Harry's use ladders ([Robinhood](https://robinhood.com/us/en/referrals), [Harry's](https://tim.blog/2014/07/21/harrys-prelaunch/)). No referral-specific RCT on ladders was found, so ship it as an A/B-able option.
12. **Reward size as a config plus an experiment, with a moderate default and LTV-based ceilings.** Bigger rewards bring more but less profitable customers ([Marketing Science 2020](https://econbiz.de/Record/referral-reward-size-and-new-customer-profitability-wolters-heike-maria/10012405758)). Rewards matter more for weaker or new brands ([J. Marketing 2007](https://kellercenter.hankamer.baylor.edu/news/story/2008/insider-referral-reward-programs-and-referral-likelihood)). Airbnb saw rewards plateau ([Lenny](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/this-week-16-building-a-referrals)). Tesla cut its program over cost ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/03/22/teslas-customer-referral-program-is-back-weeks-after-it-was-killed-off/)).

Also worth keeping but not in the top 12:

- Play Install Referrer prefill and the paste-code fallback (✔; [Android docs](https://developer.android.com/google/play/installreferrer/igetinstallreferrerservice)).
- An optional symbolic badge.
- "Invite to unlock" thresholds. These raised referrals but not revenue on a freemium platform ([Mgmt Sci 2022](https://rsm.nl/discovery/2022/engagement-dating-apps)), so use them with care in subscription apps.

## 3. Anti-patterns and backlash

| # | Anti-pattern | What happened | Source | Kit guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Uploading contacts and auto-sending reminder invites** | *Perkins v. LinkedIn* (N.D. Cal. 5:13-cv-04303). The court let a right-of-publicity claim proceed over LinkedIn's repeated follow-up emails sent in members' names. LinkedIn settled for $13M plus $3.25M in fees, without admitting wrongdoing, and changed its disclosures. | [Justia docket](https://docs.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/california/candce/5:2013cv04303/270092/106/1.html) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2015/10/05/linkedin-class-action/) · [Winston summary](https://www.winston.com/print/v2/content/60073/linkedin-agrees-to-settle-email-collection-class-action-claims.pdf) | No automated reminders sent in a user's name. Any nudge to an invited friend must be sent by the inviter, by hand, one at a time, from their own WhatsApp. |
| A2 | **Silent address-book upload** | The FTC charged that Path collected names, numbers, emails and birthdays from users' contacts on first launch without consent.
