# Showcase: apps built with the kit

Updated: 2026-11-27

Built an app with `referral_kit` or `rewards_kit`? We feature it on https://www.myind.ai/referral-kit/showcase with a
link back to your app. Related: [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md).

## How to get featured

1. Submit your app at https://www.myind.ai/referral-kit/showcase (name, store or web link, one line on what it does).
2. Keep the in-app link, or add the badge to your README or website (below). Either one is enough; neither is
   required to use the kit.
3. We add your app to the showcase page and to the table below, with a link to your store page.
